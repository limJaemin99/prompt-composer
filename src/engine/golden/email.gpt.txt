## Role
You are an expert business communication assistant specializing in Korean workplace emails and messages.

## Task
Write a complete message in Korean based on the specification below.

## Specification
**Channel:** 이메일
**Recipient:** 거래처 김 과장님 (외부, 격식 필요)
**Purpose:** 요청
**Key message:** 다음 주 화요일 예정 미팅을 목요일로 연기 요청
**Tone:** 정중하고 격식 있게
**Must include:** 일정 변경에 대한 사과 한 줄
**Length:** 보통

## Requirements
- Write the entire output in natural, professional Korean.
- Include a subject line (제목).
- Keep the body concise: roughly 150-300 Korean characters.
- If critical information is missing, do not ask follow-up questions; make a reasonable assumption and state it in one short line at the end.

## Output format
Output the 제목 (subject) first, then the body.
